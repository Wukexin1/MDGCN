Public materials
----------------
The repository contains the training, evaluation, analysis and plotting code.
The release provides prepared inputs, fixed splits, configurations, training
histories and saved predictions for all 471 runs. Original geodesic distance
files are provided through the MVGCN source folders; docs/DOWNLOADS.md explains
their placement and hash-checked import. Historical checkpoints are not
included in this release. The training programs generate new checkpoints.

Completed local verification
----------------------------
The statistics verifier checked 471 runs, 75,630 prediction rows, 630 displayed
mean/standard-deviation pairs and 30 candidate-region statistics. Figures 4
and 6 are regenerated with tools/plot_figures.py. The 320 cached distance
matrices and 471 historical checkpoints were verified against their manifests.

Four historical models were also evaluated on their full test splits:
synthetic PointNet, synthetic MDGCN1, real MDGCN2 and the real component model
with soft aggregation and add fusion at batch size 25. All 176 predicted
categories matched the retained predictions. Environment details, probability
differences and measured times are in verification/inference_summary.json.

Recorded training intervals total 49.57 hours for complete, 6.55 hours for
uniform and 21.94 hours for components. The timer boundaries are described in
docs/ENVIRONMENT.md. The local saved-result statistics check took 2.62 seconds.

The dated environment snapshots and original verification records are retained
unchanged. Availability notes in staging_manifest.json refer to the original
local preparation on 23 October 2026; this file describes the public release.
